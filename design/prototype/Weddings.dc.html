Wedding questions

How early should we book?

As early as you can. Dates are confirmed in the order retainers are paid, and I take no more than two sessions on any day.

Can we add more time on the day?

Yes. Additional shooting hours are $250 each.

What does timeline assistance mean?

In the Classic package, I help you plan the timing of the day, so there's room for portraits, family photographs and the moments that matter most to you.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel outside the DMV is quoted separately, and the quote is agreed before your date is confirmed.

Will we receive the RAW files?

No. RAW files are not included in any package. You receive fully edited images.

When will our photographs be ready?

Within 2–4 weeks of the wedding, once the remaining balance has been paid.

Is your date still open?

Choose a package and check availability. It only takes a few details to send a request.
