How payment works

  1. Retainer. A 35% non-refundable retainer reserves your date. For example, the retainer for the Classic wedding package ($3,000) is $1,050.
  2. Balance. The remaining balance is due before your final photographs are delivered.
  3. Extras. Additional services and travel may add to the total. They are agreed with you in writing before your date is confirmed.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. You'll receive a receipt by email for every payment.
